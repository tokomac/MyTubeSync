Privacy Policy

MyTubeSync (the "App") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

The App may collect and store the following information:

  • Favorite Video Information: Video IDs, titles, thumbnails, and channel information of favorited videos
  • Watch History: History of videos played within the app
  • Channel Subscriptions: Information about subscribed channels
  • Category Settings: Names, icons, and colors of created categories

2. Where Information is Stored

Collected information is stored in the following locations:

  • On Device: Stored locally using SwiftData
  • iCloud: Synced with other devices signed in with the same Apple ID through Apple iCloud

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • YouTube Data API: Used for video search and retrieving video information. YouTube's Terms of Service and Google's Privacy Policy apply.
  • Apple iCloud (CloudKit): Used for data synchronization. Apple's Privacy Policy applies.

4. Purpose of Information Use

Collected information is used for the following purposes:

  • Saving and displaying favorite videos
  • Recording and displaying watch history
  • Managing channel subscriptions
  • Syncing data across multiple devices

5. Information Sharing

The App does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties. Exceptions may apply when required by law or with user consent.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete their data through the following methods:

  • Delete individual favorites or watch history within the app
  • Uninstall the app
  • Delete app data from iCloud settings

7. Security

The App implements appropriate security measures to protect user information. Communications with iCloud are encrypted and comply with Apple's security standards.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as needed. We will notify users of significant changes within the app.
